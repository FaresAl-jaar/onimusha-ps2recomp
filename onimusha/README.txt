Onimusha: Dawn of Dreams PC (test build)

Starting the game:
1. Unpack the whole folder somewhere with at least 6 GB of free space.
2. Run OnimushaLauncher.exe.
3. On the "Game" tab, pick your Disc 1 ISO (button "ISO...").
4. Press PLAY. The first start unpacks the ISO once (folder disc1),
   after that the game starts right away.

Settings (all optional, the defaults work):
* Display: fullscreen, borderless window or window, monitor, VSync
* Graphics: renderer (gpu or cpu), internal resolution scale, aspect ratio,
  image filter, HD texture pack (the DoD2.5.zip of the Onimusha HD Remastered
  Project, picked with "ZIP...")
* Audio: master volume
* Controls: keyboard, mouse and controller are freely mappable. Every controller
  Windows knows (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch and many others) works directly.

In game:
* Alt+Enter switches between window and fullscreen.
* Close with the window button or Alt+F4.
* Saves are in the folder saves.
* onimusha.log next to Onimusha.exe records what happened (useful for bug reports).

Known limits of this test build:
* Parts of the 3D scenes are still missing.
* Music, effects and voice share one volume slider.
